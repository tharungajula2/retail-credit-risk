# Reporting-date input gate

The standalone [`reporting_input_gate.py`](../src/creditrisk/regulatory/reporting_input_gate.py) checks evidence *before* a proposed ECL or Basel illustration. It does not call or change the historical [staging](../src/creditrisk/regulatory/staging.py), [ECL](../src/creditrisk/regulatory/ecl.py) or [capital](../src/creditrisk/regulatory/basel_capital.py) engines. It makes no accounting or prudential compliance finding. The [Phase 7 audit](../../../portfolio/reviews/Retail-Credit-Risk-ECL-Basel-Audit.md) explains why those engines and saved figures remain unverified.

## Contract and reporting cutoff

Call `validate_ecl_input(record)` for a performing Stage 1/2 accounting illustration, or `validate_basel_input(record)` for a performing `irb_other_retail` or `standardised_retail` illustration. A successful call returns an immutable `ValidatedReportingInput` naming the *illustrative* purpose, reporting date, currency, exposure and PD horizon. A failed call raises `ValueError` naming the missing or incompatible evidence. These functions neither calculate nor authorise ECL, RWA, regulatory expected loss or capital.

Every record requires `loan_id`, `reporting_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), timezone-aware `reporting_at` on that date, same-day `status_observed_at`, `status_available_at`, `status_source`, explicit reporting-date `defaulted` boolean, uppercase three-letter `currency`, and `ead`. The timestamp is the cutoff; dated observations and their availability timestamps must be at or before it. An earlier observation is permitted where the methodology can use it; this gate does **not** establish acceptable staleness. A source name is an assertion awaiting authentication, not proof of a source.

`ead` has `loan_id`, `amount`, `currency`, `unit`, `basis`, `observed_at`, `available_at`, and `source`. The amount is a finite non-negative decimal string, integer or `Decimal`; floats are rejected. `unit` is explicitly `currency_units`, and the EAD currency must match the record. `observed_at <= available_at <= reporting_at`. `pd` adds `loan_id`, `value` in [0,1], `basis`, `window_start`, `window_end`, and the same observation/availability/source fields. `lgd` has `loan_id`, a [0,1] `value`, `basis`, and the same dated evidence fields. All nested loan IDs must match the reporting record. All schemas reject missing and extra fields. Amounts and probabilities are inputs, **never generated defaults**.

## Different eligibility paths

| Path | Required evidence and period | Rejected shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| ECL Stage 1 | `stage=1`, `expected_life_end`, stage and initial/current risk assessment dates/sources; prospective economic LGD; prospective default exposure; documented effective interest rate and forward-looking scenario source with observation/availability dates. `pd.basis=forward_12m`, window begins at reporting date and ends at the earlier of 12 calendar months and expected life. | An undated current status, final loan outcome, realized workout LGD, default-date balance or generic interest-rate/PD fallback. |
| ECL Stage 2 | Same evidence, with `pd.basis=forward_lifetime` and window ending at `expected_life_end`. | Reusing a 12-month PD as lifetime risk or a same-portfolio current PD as initial risk evidence. |
| Basel IRB other retail | Explicit `jurisdiction`, `rulebook_version`, rulebook effective date, availability and source; dated retail eligibility, named IRB authorisation and its availability; `pd.basis=regulatory_one_year` over the next 12 calendar months; `lgd.basis=regulatory_downturn`; `ead.basis=regulatory_default_exposure`. | Treating an accounting LGD or realized workout label as regulatory downturn LGD, or assuming IRB approval. |
| Basel standardised retail | Same named rulebook and dated retail eligibility; `ead.basis=regulatory_exposure`; `pd`, `lgd` and IRB authorisation fields must explicitly be `None`. | Applying a retail risk weight without an eligibility assertion, or mixing IRB parameters into the standardised path. |

For ECL, `stage_assessed_at` must follow same-day status and current-risk observations; `initial_risk_observed_at` must precede `current_risk_observed_at`. Stage and both risk observations also require availability timestamps. The gate requires a named `risk_comparison_source`, but it cannot verify the underlying SICR analysis. The `effective_interest_rate` is a non-negative finite input with `eir_observed_at`, `eir_available_at` and `eir_source`; scenario evidence has equivalent dates and a source. Basel retail eligibility likewise needs observation and availability timestamps. This records availability; it does not validate discounting, cash-flow timing, scenario probabilities or weights.

Both ECL Stage 3 and defaulted capital exposures fail explicitly. They need different dated cash-shortfall and regulatory default-treatment contracts; the legacy performing formulas must not be applied to them. Unknown stages/approaches and unsupported EAD bases also fail. The IRB and standardised checks do **not** verify parameter floors, risk-weight eligibility rules, approval validity, supervisory implementation or an applicable national rulebook. A string such as `jurisdiction` or `rulebook_source` must eventually be backed by an authenticated policy manifest.

## Fit with the existing foundations

The [PD target foundation](PD_TARGET_FOUNDATION.md) validates historical 12-month *labels* from dated default events and complete observation windows. It does not estimate current reporting-date PD or lifetime default risk. The [LGD/EAD data foundation](LGD_EAD_DATA_FOUNDATION.md) validates historical default balances, recoveries and workout/CCF eligibility. Its realized `ValidatedLGD`/`ValidatedEAD` records cannot be passed off as prospective or regulatory inputs. An authenticated reporting-date adapter would have to derive and document distinct parameter estimates and their availability, currency, units, horizon and sources before calling this gate. The [Phase 4 PD guide](PD_MODEL_CORRECTIONS.md) likewise makes saved historical metrics unverified.

The gate has only [synthetic boundary tests](../tests/test_reporting_input_gate.py). The exact source loan CSV, event ledger, reporting-date status panel, forecasts, EIR evidence, rulebook selection and regulatory authorisation are unavailable. No saved ECL or capital total was recalculated or authenticated. An admitted record says **its supplied evidence is internally well formed for the named illustrative path**; it does not make the historical engines safe to run, or establish IFRS 9, Ind AS 109 or Basel compliance.
