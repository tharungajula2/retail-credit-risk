Data Leakage Prevention Guardrails & Schema Hygiene
Strict Leakage Prevention Protocol (schema.py):
To guarantee regulatory compliance and eliminate future-information leakage into credit risk features, all post-origination behavior and resolution outcome columns are stripped during feature extraction. Stripped columns include: recoveries, collection_recovery_fee, total_pymnt, total_rec_prncp, last_pymnt_d, next_pymnt_d, last_credit_pull_d.

Ground-Truth Dataset Origin:
Public LendingClub consumer unsecured term loan dataset covering origination vintages 2007 through 2014. No proprietary or employer data is used.
